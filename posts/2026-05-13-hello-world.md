This is the first post on the wyatt log. The plan is simple: write
short, dated notes about what I'm learning while building
[wyatt](https://wyatterp.ai). Wins, dead ends, decisions I want to
remember six months from now.

The bar for posting here is intentionally low. If a thought is worth
five minutes of writing, it's worth a post.

### what to expect

- engineering notes from building the agent
- product decisions and the reasoning behind them
- things I got wrong, in roughly the order I got them wrong

No newsletter, no comments, no tracking. Just a list.
